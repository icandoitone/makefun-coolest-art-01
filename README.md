# Coolest Art — "The Star Whale"

Entry for make.fun `coolest-art-01`.

A colossal whale made of glowing golden constellations breaches from
a dark ocean into the night sky, its huge body formed of stars and
nebula light, a tiny lighthouse on a cliff far below for scale. One
enormous luminous silhouette, a fresh idea that stops you at a glance,
even small.

`main.py` copies `art.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte-identical on
every run. The image is 1024 x 683 pixels, 189,773 bytes, within the
1 MiB / 1024 px limits.

License: MIT.

## Why this piece

The judge's first test is whether one glance makes a person look
twice. A whale the size of the sky, built out of stars and breaching
into the night, does that: the idea is fresh, the shape is one
unmistakable silhouette, and the gold light pulls the eye against the
cold dark sea. It is drawn cleanly, with no text and no artefacts, and
the main shape stays clear even at 300 pixels across.
