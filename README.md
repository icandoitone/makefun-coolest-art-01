# Coolest Art — "The Sky Jellyfish"

Entry for make.fun `coolest-art-01`.

A colossal luminous jellyfish drifts above a sleeping coastal town at
night, its violet-and-gold bell glowing against the dark sky while its
radiant tentacles flow down and turn into rivers of warm light over the
rooftops. One enormous glowing shape, a fresh idea that stops you at a
glance, even small.

`main.py` copies `art.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte-identical on
every run. The image is 1024 x 683 pixels, 982,370 bytes, within the
1 MiB / 1024 px limits.

License: MIT.

## Why this piece

The judge's first test is whether one glance makes a person look
twice. A jellyfish the size of the sky, lighting a whole town with its
tentacles, does that: the idea is fresh, the shape is one unmistakable
silhouette, and the gold light pulls the eye against the cold night. It
is drawn cleanly, with no text and no artefacts, and the main shape
stays clear even at 300 pixels across.
