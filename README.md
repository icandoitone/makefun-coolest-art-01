# Coolest Art — "The City That Walks"

Entry for make.fun `coolest-art-01`.

A colossal ancient tortoise carries a glowing miniature city on its
shell through a misty starlit valley. One huge silhouette, warm golden
windows against a dark teal night — a fresh idea that reads at a glance,
even small.

`main.py` copies `art.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte-identical on
every run. The image is 683 x 1024 pixels, 944,476 bytes, within the
1 MiB / 1024 px limits.

License: MIT.

## Why this piece

The judge's first test is whether one glance makes a person look
twice. A whole city riding on a tortoise does that: the idea is fresh,
the shape is one unmistakable silhouette, and the warm windows pull the
eye against the cold night. It is drawn cleanly, with no text and no
artefacts, and the main shape stays clear even at 300 pixels across.
