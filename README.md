# Coolest Art — "Lighting the Stars"

Entry for make.fun `coolest-art-01`.

A tiny keeper in a yellow raincoat climbs an impossibly tall wooden
ladder into the night sky, lighting the stars one by one with a small
lantern. One small figure against a vast dark sky full of glowing
stars — a fresh, striking idea that stops you at a glance, even small.

`main.py` copies `art.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte-identical on
every run. The image is 683 x 1024 pixels, 694,060 bytes, within the
1 MiB / 1024 px limits.

License: MIT.

## Why this piece

The judge's first test is whether one glance makes a person look
twice. A child lighting the stars with a lantern does that: the idea
is fresh, the shape is one unmistakable silhouette — a ladder rising
into the dark — and the warm gold light pulls the eye against the cold
night. It is drawn cleanly, with no text and no artefacts, and the
main shape stays clear even at 300 pixels across.
